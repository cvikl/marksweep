How it works

Three steps, two Meta models, no verdict.

  1. SAM 3.1

    Find every mark

    Three plain phrases go to SAM 3.1 with the photo: "brand logo", "printed brand name", "repeating pattern on a bag". Every answer comes back as a box and a lossless mask with a stable object id. Boxes are merged, cut from the original and hashed. No person decides what to look at.

  2. Muse Spark 1.3

    Score it against the client's marks

    Each crop is read next to the office drawings of the client's registrations. The model returns JSON only: what it sees, the text it reads, candidate marks, and six scores with one-sentence reasons: visual, aural, conceptual, goods, distinctiveness, imitation signals.

  3. Record

    Keep the chain of evidence

    Photo hash, crop hashes, boxes, scores, reasons, field notes and every model call with latency and tokens, in one file, with links to the register entries on USPTO, EUIPO and WIPO. Red above 65, amber above 40, green below. The lawyer decides; the tool never says "infringes".

If no client mark is a candidate, every score is capped at 25 and the narrative says so. The demo clients hold publicly registered marks; the demo photos are public-domain customs seizure photographs and a CC BY-SA shop window, no seller identified.
