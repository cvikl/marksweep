How it works

Segment

SAM 3.1 (Meta Model API, Responses API, streamed) is asked three short noun phrases, "brand logo", "printed brand name", "repeating pattern on a bag". Every box and lossless mask comes back with a stable object id; boxes are merged across phrases and cut from the original photo. No model picks what to look at; the segmenter finds everything that looks like a mark.

Assess

Muse Spark 1.3 sees each crop next to the office drawings of the client's registered marks and returns schema-checked JSON: what it sees, the text it reads, which client marks are candidates, six sub-scores with rationales (visual, aural, conceptual, goods, distinctiveness, imitation signals), a narrative in "highly concerning / worth checking / low concern" language, never a legal conclusion.

Record

Photo hash, crop hashes, boxes, masks, scores, rationales and the ledger of every model call (model, purpose, latency, tokens) in one JSON file per sweep, served read-only. The roll-up is a weighted mean with N/A weights redistributed, same bands as Signum, our case-assessment product: red at 65, amber at 40.

What it is not

Not a verdict. The register entries are the only comparison; the scores are research signals for the lawyer's own global assessment. The two demo clients use publicly registered marks; the demo photos are public-domain customs seizure photographs and a CC BY-SA shop window, no seller is identified.
