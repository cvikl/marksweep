# Demo scene prompts for muse-image-1.0

Synthetic field photos so no real seller is pictured. Each prompt asks for a realistic phone photo of a stall
or shelf with imitation goods bearing look-alike marks. Rendered with `marksweep render "<prompt>" fixtures/demos/<name>/photo.png`.

1. lego-stall: "Realistic smartphone photo, slightly tilted, of a crowded night-market toy stall in Hong Kong. Stacked colourful brick-set boxes with large glossy logos: some boxes carry a white word on a red rounded square with yellow and black border, others a very similar red square logo reading LEPIN, another reading LEGE. Price tags in HKD handwritten on cardboard. Fluorescent light, busy background, shallow depth of field."
2. lv-stall: "Realistic smartphone photo of a street-market handbag stall under a tarpaulin at dusk. Dozens of brown handbags and wallets with a repeating monogram canvas pattern of interlocking letters, four-petal flowers and quatrefoils, some with slightly altered letters like LW and NV. A paper sign says 'Genuine leather 150'. Natural light, candid, nobody's face visible."
3. lego-shelf: "Realistic photo of a discount toy shop shelf, eye level, boxes of building-brick sets with imitation logos: a red square with white letters BELA, one with LELE, one with the exact LEGO look but text QMAN. Shelf price labels, fluorescent lighting, some boxes dusty."
